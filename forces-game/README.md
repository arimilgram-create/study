# Inertia Quest

A study game for the **Forces, Free Body Diagrams and First Law** quiz review and the
**Balanced Forces and Constant Velocity** test review. Each station is a review question turned into a mini-game.

**Lock In (show your work):** every calculation makes you (1) pick the equation, (2) plug in the
values with units, (3) answer with units. You can't skip to the answer, and you build a full-work streak.

### Test Review: Balanced Forces & Constant Velocity

| Station | Test question | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| Velocity Is a Vector | Q1 | Drag lines on x-t and v-t graphs, write a velocity problem |
| Tom the Cat | Q2 | Weight, lifting at constant velocity, weight on Pluto |
| Graph Detective | Q3, Q4, Q6 | Pick graphs, draw a constant-velocity graph for Victoria's cart |
| Jayla's Boat | Q5 | Pick the FBD, time across the lake, draw x-t and v-t graphs |
| Race Day | Q7, Q10, Q11 | Race sim, time/speed ratios, shuttle to the Moon |
| Desk Normal Force | Q8 | Normal force, then pushing down with -15 N |
| Larry's Cart | Q9 | Read a v-t graph, displacement, force, draw the position graph |
| RC Car Graphs | Q9b | FBD, draw the v-t graph, displacement vs distance, change in velocity |
| Truck & Soccer Ball | Q12 | Inertia sim with ground and truck cameras |
| Graph Gauntlet | Q13 | Average speed vs average velocity, draw the position graph |
| Constant Velocity Showdown | All | Timed test-day boss, Lock In calculations |

### Quiz Review: Forces, FBDs & First Law

| Station | Review question | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| Mass or Weight? | Q1 | Speed-sort clues, then calculate F<sub>g</sub> = mg |
| Banana Scale | Q2 | Build the FBD, calculate weight, run a live force-probe graph |
| FBD Hangar | Q3 | Build FBDs for 3 planes (parked, taxiing, cruising) |
| Moon Breakout | Q4 | Cut Earth's gravity and aim the Moon at a satellite |
| Planet Slope Lab | Q5 | Weight vs. mass graphs on 4 worlds; slope = g |
| Crash Test Dummy | Q6 | Crash a car with and without a seatbelt |
| Franky's Wagon | Q7 | Tug lab, FBD, friction and net force calculations |
| Inertia or Force? | Q8 | Myth-busting sort round |
| Terry's Heavy Box | Q9 | Lift a box onto a shelf without smashing it |
| Mr. C's Desk | Extra 1 | Desk FBD, applied force and friction, desk-shoving lab |
| Mars & the Platypus | Extra 2 | Weight on Mars, a planet scale showing g depends on location |
| Magnus's Barbell | Extra 3 | Lift/hold/lower lab, then rank six forces with < and = |
| Newton's Final Exam | All | 10 random timed questions, 3 hearts |

Miss a question twice and the game shows you the right answer, with the worked explanation.

**Cases, casino and character:** XP you earn (or casino chips) buys cases in the Case Shop (Rest, Velocity,
Momentum and Newton) holding 67 cosmetics across 5 rarities, with shiny variants. Open 1 or 5 at a time.
Trade items in for chips at the Casino and play Gravity Plinko, Rocket Crash or Coin Flip (play chips only). Build your character in the Character screen and equip items
from your locker. Rank is based on total XP earned, so spending never lowers it.

Open `index.html` in a browser to play. Progress (XP, stars) is saved in the browser.
