# Inertia Quest

A study game for the **Forces, Free Body Diagrams and First Law** quiz review and the
**Balanced Forces and Constant Velocity** test review. Each station is a review question turned into a mini-game.

**Lock In (show your work):** every calculation makes you (1) pick the equation, (2) plug in the
values with units, (3) answer with units. You can't skip to the answer, and you build a full-work streak.

### Acceleration Packet (start here)

Everything from the acceleration packets, built from the unit summary.

| Station | What it covers |
|---|---|
| Accelerating = Changing Velocity | Speeding up, slowing down, and turning; a = Δv / t with units (Ima Speedin, Porsche) |
| Which Way Is the Acceleration? | Direction of a when speeding up vs. slowing down; Billy; the softball |
| Speeding Up or Slowing Down? | Read four v-t graphs: velocity sign, speeding up or slowing, acceleration sign |
| Acceleration from a v-t Graph | Slope on two parts of a graph, with the sign |
| Acceleration Word Problems | Boat, softball, Anna, Tesla, Regina, Gronk (answers match the key) |
| Fan Cart Lab | Predict the v-t graph, then run it: trials A to E, modeled on your lab sheet |
| Newton's 2nd Law Calcs | ΣF = ma, proportional reasoning (Henry, dump truck, Nathan & Carla), friction, skydiver |
| Newton's 2nd Law: Fill in the Blanks | Elephant, parachute, car, wheelbarrow, sled, cart from the Physics Classroom figures |
| FBD Walkaround | Parachute, sliding block, cart and hanging mass, ball, string: speeding up or slowing down |
| Classic Free Body Diagrams | Book, crate, sledder, ball at its peak, elevator, crate on rough floor |

Notes: the fan cart is a model (a = 0.25 × force, while the fan is on), not your real cart data. The
walkaround and classic FBD sheets have no answer key, so those answers come from the physics. The
Physics Classroom blanks use the figures as drawn; a car with only friction is treated as having a net
force of friction alone.

**Loot and perks (v2):** cases now drop an item plus sometimes a bonus bag of chips. An epic or better
is guaranteed every 15 cases without one (the pity meter). Items you wear give small perks: hats and
titles add XP from studying, outfits and sidekicks add chips from bonus bags and trade-ins. Shiny
versions double the perk. Opening shows the case shake, the lid, the reel, and a reveal that gets bigger
for rarer drops. x5 and x10 open as a card grid.

**Casino (v2):** Plinko has 8, 12 or 16 rows and low, medium or high risk, with the expected return
shown (96% on every setting). Mines has 1 to 20 mines. Coin Ladder doubles down on each correct call.
Chips convert to spendable XP at 1 chip = 1 XP (rank still comes from studying).

**Vector art (v3):** `js/art.js` holds 524 vector items from [OpenMoji](https://openmoji.org), licensed
CC BY-SA 4.0. They include 40 outfits, 7 hats, 126 animal sidekicks, and 351 charms. Hats, outfits, and
sidekicks appear on your character. Every item shows its vector in the locker, shop, and drop cards.
The art is generated from the OpenMoji color SVG set, so don't edit it by hand.

**Cheaper cases (v3):** Pocket Case (40 XP or 30 chips) and Lab Case (90 XP or 50 chips). Both still
return less than they cost, even with the perk bonus maxed.

**Cases, casino and character:** XP you earn (or casino chips) buys cases in the Case Shop (Rest, Velocity,
Momentum and Newton) holding 67 cosmetics across 5 rarities, with shiny variants. Open 1 or 5 at a time.
Trade items in for chips at the Casino and play Gravity Plinko, Rocket Crash or Coin Flip (play chips only). Build your character in the Character screen and equip items
from your locker. Rank is based on total XP earned, so spending never lowers it.

Open `index.html` in a browser to play. Progress (XP, stars) is saved in the browser.
